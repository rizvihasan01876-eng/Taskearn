Frequently asked questions

Is TaskEarn free to join?

Yes. Registration is free, and TaskEarn never asks you to pay to access tasks.

How are rewards verified?

Each provider confirms task completion on their end and sends that confirmation to TaskEarn before a reward is credited to your wallet.

Why is a reward showing as pending?

Some providers take time to verify a completion. The reward appears once that verification is received — this can take from minutes to a few days depending on the provider.

When can I withdraw?

Once your balance meets the minimum withdrawal amount shown in your wallet, you can submit a withdrawal request for review.

How does the referral program work?

You get a unique referral link. When someone registers through it and later completes a qualifying task, you receive a 10% commission on that reward.
